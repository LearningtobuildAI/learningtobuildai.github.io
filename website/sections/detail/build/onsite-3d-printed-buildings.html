PART 1Executive Overview
1Definition

On-site 3D-printed buildings involve the construction of structures using large-scale 3D printers to lay down layers of material directly at the building site. This technology is particularly suited for creating walls and other load-bearing elements.

PART 2Technical & Manufacturing
4How It Works

A specialized concrete mix is pumped through a nozzle controlled by a robotic arm or gantry system, which follows a digital design to print out walls layer by layer. Openings and conduits are pre-planned into the design to ensure proper integration with plumbing, electrical, and other services.

5Materials Used
  • specialized concrete mix
  • 3D printing ink
6Manufacturing / Creation Process

Manufacturing involves setting up a large-scale 3D printer capable of handling specialized concrete mixes. The printer needs to be calibrated accurately to ensure precise layer-by-layer printing, which can take several days or weeks depending on the size of the structure being printed.

7Build Process

The build process starts with digital design and planning, followed by setting up the 3D printer at the construction site. The printer then extrudes layers of concrete mix, creating walls and other structural elements layer by layer. Once printing is complete, additional work such as finishing, plumbing, and electrical installations are carried out.

14Glossary
3D-printed building
A structure constructed using a large-scale 3D printer to lay down layers of material directly at the construction site.
gantry system
A type of robotic arm or frame used in 3D printing for buildings, providing a stable platform for extruding materials layer by layer.
specialized concrete mix
A customized blend of materials designed to be suitable for use with 3D printers in construction, often containing additives that improve printability and structural properties.
