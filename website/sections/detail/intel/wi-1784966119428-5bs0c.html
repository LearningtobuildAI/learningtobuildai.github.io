1Definition

Civilisation-Scale Engineering (CSE) refers to the conceptual and practical application of advanced technologies and methodologies aimed at altering or creating large-scale structures and systems that can affect entire civilisations or even planetary bodies. This includes projects such as Dyson spheres, orbital rings, stellar lifting, and other ambitious space engineering initiatives.

2How It Works

CSE involves the use of advanced materials science, energy management, and computational modeling to design and implement large-scale structures in space. These structures are typically intended to harness or manipulate vast amounts of resources for energy production, habitat creation, or other significant purposes. The processes involve complex simulations and theoretical frameworks that predict the structural integrity, environmental impact, and resource requirements of such projects.

3Problem It Solves

CSE aims to address issues related to sustainable energy supply, space exploration, and the long-term expansion of human civilisation beyond Earth. It seeks to solve problems associated with resource scarcity, energy limitations, and the need for advanced habitats in space or on other celestial bodies.

4Manufacturing / Creation Process

The manufacturing processes for CSE projects are highly complex and involve cutting-edge technologies such as nanofactories, additive manufacturing, and automated assembly systems. The materials used must be capable of withstanding extreme conditions in space, including vacuum, radiation, and temperature fluctuations.

6Build Process

CSE projects require a multi-step build process that includes initial design phases, material synthesis, construction, and deployment. Each phase involves extensive testing and validation to ensure the structural integrity and operational efficiency of the final product.

16Glossary
Dyson sphere
A hypothetical megastructure that completely encases a star to capture its energy. It represents one of the most ambitious concepts in CSE.
Orbital ring
A structure built around Earth or another celestial body, designed to support large-scale infrastructure and potentially serve as a platform for space-based industries.
Stellar lifting
The theoretical process of moving stellar material into orbit around a star, often proposed as a means to access the immense energy contained within stars.
Galactic engineering
The concept of altering or creating large-scale structures on a galactic scale, potentially including the manipulation of entire star systems for civilisation expansion.
