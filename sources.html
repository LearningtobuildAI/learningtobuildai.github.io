Evidence review

A source name or working link does not verify a claim. A reviewed summary records the exact claim, company role, supporting primary source and review date. A changed claim must be reviewed again.

“Summary source-reviewed” applies to the short summary and the company roles explicitly supported by its citations. It is not an independent test of a manufacturer's performance claims. Unverified entries are awaiting review and must not be treated as established facts.

Research institutions, manufacturers, customers and investors have different roles. An organisation's existence alone does not prove it makes the technology. Proposed products, laboratory research, trials and deployed products must remain distinct.

Explainer videos

Explainers use narrated diagrams and labelled animations. These are illustrations, not proof of a product or footage of a factory. The transcript links to the sources. Public descriptions of production do not constitute proprietary manufacturing instructions.

Published snapshots and engines

The static public site serves published snapshots. A local engine being active when an export was made does not establish a live connection on this website. Chat and other server-only functions must report unavailable unless connected.

Source Classes

  • Primary scientific and technical publications (publicly accessible does not mean public domain)
  • Open technology announcements and reports
  • Manufacturer documentation, research institutions and regulator publications
